# Little Loggers — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Little Loggers ("the app", "we") handles your information.

## Summary

Little Loggers is an offline-first iOS app. We do not collect, receive, store, or share any personal information about you. Your data stays on your device — and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud container.

## What Data the App Handles

When you use Little Loggers, you may enter:

- Children's names, birthdates, and notes
- Caregiver names
- Logs of diapers, feedings, sleep, medications, and pumping sessions
- Milk inventory entries

This information is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework.

If you are signed into iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled for Little Loggers, the same information is synchronized across your own Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit **private database**. This data is stored in a container associated with your Apple ID. The developer of Little Loggers cannot access, view, or download any data in your private CloudKit container.

## What We Do NOT Do

- We do **not** operate any servers that receive your data.
- We do **not** use any third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
- We do **not** track you across apps or websites.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or share any user data with anyone.
- The app does **not** require an account or login.

## Data You Choose to Export

Little Loggers includes a Backup feature that produces a `.babylogbackup` file containing your logs. This file is generated and saved by you, on your device. Anything you do with that file (share via AirDrop, email, cloud drive, etc.) is entirely under your control and outside the scope of this policy.

## Children's Privacy

Little Loggers is intended for use by parents, guardians, and caregivers. The app does not knowingly collect any information directly from children. Information about a child (such as a name or birthdate) is entered by the caregiver and stored only as described above.

## Your Rights and Controls

- **Delete data on this device:** delete the Little Loggers app from your iPhone.
- **Delete iCloud-synced data:** Settings → [your Apple ID] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Little Loggers → Delete Data, or sign out of iCloud.
- **Export your data:** open the app → Settings → Data → Backup.

Because we do not have access to your data, we cannot delete it on your behalf — only you can.
