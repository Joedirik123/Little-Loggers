## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Little Loggers work without an internet connection?**
Yes. All your logs are stored on-device. Internet is only needed if you want to sync across your own devices via iCloud.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device by default. If you're signed into iCloud, you can opt in to sync via Apple's private iCloud container. Your data is never sent to any third party.

**How do I export my data?**
Open the app → Settings → Data → Backup → export.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete the app from your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, also sign out of iCloud or delete the app's iCloud data via Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage → Little Loggers.
